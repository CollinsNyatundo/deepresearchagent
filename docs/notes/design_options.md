## 3. Marketing Landing Page Copy (Module A)

Here is the proposed copy structure for **DeepResearch.ai** landing page, optimized for developers and researchers:

### Hero Headline
> **"Deep Web Intelligence. Synthesized in Seconds."**
> *Stop reading page-by-page. Let our autonomous agent swarm search, filter relevance, crawl sidebars, bypass paywalls, and build a verified knowledge graph for your most complex queries.*

### Subtext
> `npm install -g @deepresearch/cli`
> *Configure your LLM provider (Groq, OpenRouter) and run deep research directly from your browser or terminal.*

### Feature Core: The Three Pillars
1. **Agentic Cascades:** Automatic intent classification routing through Tavily, Exa, and Parallel AI. Failing search queries trigger auto-fallbacks to secondary engines.
2. **Resilient Scraping:** Dual-tier Stealthy Camoufox and Playwright fetchers bypass Cloudflare and scraping guards, filtering boilerplate nodes to retain only raw semantic data.
3. **Structured Citations & Graphify:** Inline report citations dynamically mapped to original sources, coupled with an interactive G6 mindmap community visualizer.
